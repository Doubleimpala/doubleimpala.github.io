Role of Surface Tension in Microrobot Penetration in Membranes

Membrane-penetrating microrobots are a promising way to deliver bioactive compounds into cells, but the fluid dynamics of the penetration itself, once fusion begins, hadn't been well studied. This work looks at how far a microrobot droplet penetrates into a liquid bath, and finds that it depends on the surface tension difference between the droplet and the bath, the curvature of the connecting bridge, and the droplet's size, a physical model for how microrobots could push through cell membranes.
